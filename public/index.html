Connect your football-data.org account

  1. Create a free account at football-data.org and obtain your API token.
  2. In your Vercel project, go to Settings → Environment Variables.
  3. Add a variable named FOOTBALL_DATA_API_KEY with your token as the value, then redeploy.

The key never reaches the browser — it's read server-side by the /api/live function.
